FIND IT FIRST

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Find It First ("we," "our," or "the app") helps users search for marketplace listings, save searches called Hunts, and receive notifications about matching items. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and disclosed.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Find It First, we may collect:

  • Account information: Email address, account identifiers, and authentication information.
  • Location information: City or ZIP code, and precise GPS coordinates when you choose to use your device's location.
  • Search activity: Hunt keywords, search preferences, geographic radius, saved listings, matches, and listing feedback.
  • Device and notification information: Device identifiers or push notification tokens used to deliver notifications.
  • Technical information: App activity, search diagnostics, and records used to maintain service reliability and security.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Search for marketplace listings based on your preferences.
  • Provide nearby results and location-based searches.
  • Save Hunts and identify matching listings.
  • Deliver notifications you have enabled.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the app.
  • Protect the service against abuse and unauthorized access.

3. Location Information

Find It First allows you to enter a city or ZIP code instead of using your device's location. If you choose "Use my location," the app requests location permission and obtains GPS coordinates to perform nearby searches.

Location access is optional. You can change the app's location permission in your device settings. Location information may be associated with saved Hunts or search records.

4. Service Providers and Third Parties

Find It First uses external hosting, database, marketplace-data, and notification services to operate its features. Information needed to perform these functions may be processed by those providers.

Marketplace listings may originate from third-party platforms. When you open an external marketplace listing, that platform's own privacy practices apply.

We do not sell personal information to advertisers.

5. Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, and disclosure. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

Account-related information is generally retained while your account is active or as needed to provide requested features. Certain information may be retained longer where necessary to comply with legal obligations, investigate abuse, or maintain security and operational records.

7. Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data from the Profile section of the app or by contacting our support email.

Account deletion is intended to remove your account profile, saved Hunts, matches, saved listings, notification records, and associated account activity. Shared marketplace listing information that is not specific to your account may remain.

For deletion instructions, visit our Account & Data Deletion page.

8. Your Choices

You can choose whether to grant location permission, adjust supported notification preferences, manage saved Hunts, and request deletion of your account.

9. Children's Privacy

Find It First is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request and take appropriate action.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app and its services change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

11. Contact Us

For privacy questions or account-deletion requests, contact: sosapps.support@gmail.com .
