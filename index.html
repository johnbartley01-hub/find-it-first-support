Account & Data Deletion

This page explains how to request deletion of your Find It First account and associated personal data.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Find It First and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion request.

Account deletion is permanent. You will lose access to your saved Hunts and account information.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email sosapps.support@gmail.com with the subject "Find It First Account Deletion". Send the request from the email address associated with your account when possible.

We may request additional verification before processing an email deletion request to protect your account.

What data is deleted?

When your account is deleted, Find It First removes account information and associated app data, including:

  • Account profile and email address
  • Saved searches (Hunts) and their matches
  • Saved listings and listing feedback
  • Notification records and registered devices
  • Account-linked search activity and analytics records

What data may be retained?

Shared marketplace listing information that is not specific to your account may remain in the service. Information may also be retained where required by applicable law, security obligations, or legitimate recordkeeping requirements.

Any information retained for such purposes will be kept only for as long as necessary for the applicable purpose or legal requirement.

Need help?

Contact sosapps.support@gmail.com for questions about account or data deletion.
